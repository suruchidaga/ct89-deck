InstrumentActualTargetTo reach target Ceiling (score band) StatusΔ at target

Targets and ceilings are in lots (+ long, − short). The score uses the same maths as Net bias, grouped by instrument; options use the average delta of your current lots in that bucket, or ±0.5 if you hold none. Ceilings move with prices and deltas. This is a planning aid: nothing is sent to Kite.

Adjust to a target bias score

These are calculated ways to reach the score you set, not recommendations. Order of preference: options first (closing existing options, then new ones), then NG Mini futures for what's left. Deltas use Black-76 at each option's IV and change as prices move. Prices are current LTPs or fair values; check the bid/ask and liquidity before trading. Nothing here is sent to Kite.
